Documenting 2,700 Years of Silk Road Heritage: The Shakhrisabz Digital Expedition

An open-access cultural heritage archive documenting ancient Timurid monuments, sacred epigraphy, vernacular architecture, and living craft traditions in Kashkadarya, Uzbekistan. Field-documented by 20 youth stewards under a Wikimedia Foundation grant to bridge digital preservation gaps along Central Asia's historic Silk Roads.

01. Documentary Film & Field Narrative

The Shakhrisabz Field Expedition

Documentary footage capturing the multi-stage youth expedition from regional training at IT Park to exhaustive on-site monument surveys across the historic city.

“Although born in G'uzor, many of my relatives live in Shakhrisabz, and my grandfather guided me through these monumental landmarks since childhood. When our team applied for the Wikimedia grant, we had options to document heavily visited cities like Tashkent or Samarkand. I passionately chose Shakhrisabz because despite being an inscribed UNESCO World Heritage city, research showed that fewer than 6% of our historic settlements had adequate online representation. As Organization Manager and local on-the-ground guide, I led 20 peers through the city to ensure our Silk Road heritage is preserved openly for future generations.”

03. Fieldwork Methodology & Operations

A Three-Phase Digital Archiving Methodology

How our 20-member youth cohort executed a professional heritage documentation project under the Wikimedia Foundation's grant framework.

Phase 01 • 阶段一
Archival Training & Standards

Conducted a rigorous 2-day technical workshop at Karshi State University and Karshi IT Park. Delegates were trained in RAW capture, geometric perspective correction, EXIF metadata preservation, and Creative Commons open-licensing requirements.

Phase 02 • 阶段二
On-Site Expeditionary Survey

Navigated 20 researchers across Shakhrisabz's UNESCO core and Miraki alpine river basins. Documented fragile majolica glazed tiles, sacred epigraphic drum inscriptions, traditional woodcarvings, and local craft bazaar economies.

Phase 03 • 阶段三
Open Repository Ingestion

Curated and uploaded over 500 high-resolution photographic assets with trilingual metadata (English, Uzbek, Russian) to Wikimedia Commons under CC BY-SA 4.0, directly bridging Shakhrisabz's digital presence gap on global knowledge platforms.
